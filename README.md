## 📌 About the project

Germani Jewellery Academy is the business of **Michel Germani**, a Sydney jeweller and Graduate Gemologist (G.G., G.I.A.) with fifty years in the industry. He founded the Germani brand in Australia in 1985. The website brings together three parts of his business: his **history**, his **jewellery collection** and his **Academy**, a course that teaches people how to open and run a jewellery store.

The client wanted a refined, luxury look that follows his printed brochure. The site needed to:

- present the collection elegantly, with no online shop: enquiries and appointments only
- tell Michel Germani's story and the history of the Germani stores
- introduce the Academy and publish its full prospectus
- make it easy to book a showroom appointment

I am responsible for the whole build: **design, development, content structure, image optimisation, SEO and deployment**, working through the client's feedback and brand rules.

## ✨ Features

| Area | What I built |
|---|---|
| 🎨 **Brand & design** | A luxury look taken from the client's brochure: lapis-blue header and footer, ivory pages, navy text, and a metallic gold "G-diamond" logo that I traced into a vector (SVG) |
| 🏛️ **Three doors** | A home page built around the three parts of the business: the Foundation, the Collection and the Academy |
| 👤 **Foundation** | A history page telling Michel Germani's fifty-year story, with photos of the Germani stores |
| 💍 **Collections** | A gallery in five chapters (important necklaces, rare gems, diamond necklaces & bracelets, engagement rings, earrings & more) with **category filters** and a **lightbox** |
| ✏️ **Bespoke** | The step-by-step bespoke process, from first conversation to finished piece, with design sketches |
| 🎓 **Academy** | An introduction to the course and the **full 16-page prospectus** as a web page, with a PDF download |
| 📅 **Appointments** | An appointment form (Netlify Forms) plus direct phone, email and Instagram links |
| 🪪 **Digital business card** | A private business-card page with a **vCard "Save Contact"** download, a **QR code** and a print-ready card PDF |
| 📱 **Responsive** | Mobile-first layouts with a slide-out menu |
| ⚡ **Performance** | All photos converted to optimised **WebP**, with no framework or build step, so pages load fast |
| 🔍 **SEO & security** | Meta and Open Graph tags, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, security headers, and private files blocked from public access |

## 🛠️ Tech stack

- **Frontend:** semantic HTML5, modern CSS (custom properties, Flexbox and Grid), vanilla JavaScript
- **Images:** optimised WebP photos, SVG logo
- **Hosting & deployment:** Netlify, with continuous deployment from a private GitHub repository
- **Forms:** Netlify Forms for appointment requests
- **Tools:** a small Python script that builds the business-card QR code and print-ready PDF

---

## 🧭 My process

1. **Discovery:** gathered the brochure, prospectus, product photos and the client's brand rules.
2. **Design:** matched the brochure's lapis, ivory, navy and gold, and rebuilt the logo as a clean vector.
3. **Build:** produced a lightweight static site with no framework overhead.
4. **Content:** organised the collection into chapters and turned the 16-page prospectus into a web page.
5. **Iteration:** working through rounds of client feedback on content, layout and imagery.
6. **Launch:** domain, final SEO and Google setup still to come.

## 🔒 Why there's no source code here

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is a showcase repository. It contains only screenshots and this write-up.**
>
> Germani Jewellery Academy is a **real client project**. The client owns the source code, and it is kept in a **private repository** because it contains:
> - the client's business content, product photography, branding assets and prospectus
> - configuration used by the site (forms, deployment)
> - personal contact details used for the digital business card
>
> Making that repository public would expose the client's data, so I keep it private. This repo explains what I built and how.
